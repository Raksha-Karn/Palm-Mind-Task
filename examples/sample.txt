Sample interview guide

The backend engineering interview lasts 45 minutes. Candidates should prepare to discuss
Python, REST APIs, vector search, and relational databases.

The take-home assignment requires a document ingestion API and a conversational RAG API.
Accepted file formats are PDF and TXT. The assistant should cite document sources in its answers.

Interview bookings collect the candidate's name, email, date, and time. A booking is saved
only after the candidate confirms the details. The booking timezone is Asia/Kathmandu.
